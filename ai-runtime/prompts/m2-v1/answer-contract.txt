Every Answer fact has only region_id, quote, value, year, unit, entity. value is an exact decimal string; quote must be a verbatim substring of that region's text. You may leave facts empty and cite the observed row when a structured claim cannot be represented. Follow the existing action variants exactly. No confidence, validation verdict or publication fields are allowed. These are generic protocol constraints, not source evidence.
